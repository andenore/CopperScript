11 / WHY IT SUITS AI AGENTS

An agent makes good decisions when every decision gets fast, located and honest feedback.

CopperScript turns board design into that loop: text the agent can write, facts it can retrieve, and a compiler that answers in seconds.

This is the central claim about AI. Language models are good at writing structured text and at reacting to precise feedback. They are bad at guessing facts they cannot see and at judging their own work. CopperScript is built around that: give the agent text to write, facts to retrieve instead of guess, and checks that answer quickly, point at the exact line and never pretend a missing input passed.
