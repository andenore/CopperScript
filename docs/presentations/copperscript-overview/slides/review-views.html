CopperReview turns one compiled design into the views a reviewer needs, all from the electrical IR: no layout is required and nothing is drawn by hand. The pure schematic gives generated sheets, with passives folded onto the pins they serve and buses as named nets. The power topology shows every rail from its source through regulators and switches to its loads, with voltages and enables, and a declared power state such as SLEEP marks the rails that are off. The bus topology shows each configured peripheral as a named bus between devices, with its signal nets, pins and datasheet references. Selecting a part or net in one view selects it in the others and on the board, and notes attached to any of them export for the next agent run. All three pictures are the full-vertical example.
