03 / HOW THE LANGUAGE WORKS

A small set of typed building blocks.

BlockWhat it declares
part · deviceA component’s pins, domains, directions, ratings and roles; a device’s silicon pads, power domains and peripheral mux options
componentOne placed instance of a part, with its value, footprint and relationships such as decouples = U1.VDD
net · supplyConnections, and the rails that power them, with voltage and current budgets
moduleA reusable circuit with ports, instantiated on a board or simulated alone
constraint · mechanicalOutline, holes, keepouts, placement, routing rules, antennas and emission standards
power_state · benchSteady-state rail scenarios, and simulation benches with measures and limits
physical_macro · panel · buildReusable hard layouts, fabrication panels and build settings, in source
The vocabulary is small. Parts and devices describe what a component is. Components, nets and supplies describe the board. Modules make circuits reusable. Constraints and mechanical blocks carry physical intent. Power states and benches carry analysis. Macros, panels and build blocks carry layout and manufacturing settings. All of it compiles into inspectable JSON intermediate representations.
