24 / WHAT IT DOES NOT CLAIM

Evidence, not approval.

ResultWhat it does not establish
Clean ERC and native DRCElectrical, RF, power, assembly or CAM qualification
A routed boardThat the router will close another design: routing is experimental
Passing simulation benchesBoard behaviour: layout parasitics are not in the benches yet
EMI screeningEMC compliance: it ranks likely sources and never reports a pass
A synthesized antennaPerformance in the enclosure: bench tuning and testing remain

This honesty is part of what makes the tool safe for agents: a result says exactly what was checked, so nothing unverified looks finished.

Every result has a scope, and the project states it. A prototype, lab measurements and compliance testing are still required. For an agent this matters a lot: it cannot mistake a clean check for a finished product, because reports name what they did not check and missing evidence remains visible.
