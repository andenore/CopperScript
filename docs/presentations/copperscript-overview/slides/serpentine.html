A real route-board run of a two-lane fixture modelled on the router’s own serpentine test: two 0.2/0.2 mm differential pairs between pads at the pair pitch, one lane 28 mm and the other 24 mm. Without the length_match group they route straight. With it, and tuning_style set to serpentine, the router reports the group as tuned: lane B gained 4.000 mm in a four-leg serpentine with legs up to 0.91 mm high, the group skew went from 4.000 mm to zero, and because both members of the pair bend together their own skew is unchanged. The native DRC passed. Both pictures are copper render drawings of the native plots. The routing rule is abridged; the full source is in the deck’s sources/serpentine-lanes folder.
