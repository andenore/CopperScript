Keyboard and mouse shortcuts
Scroll to zoom; right-drag to pan. Click a footprint to select it; Shift-click toggles a group. Focus the board: arrows move by one snap (Shift: ten); R / Shift+R rotates; L locks temporarily, Shift+L or U unlocks; S flips sides; Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z (or Y) undo/redo temporary placement; +/− zoom; F/Home fits. Escape cancels a gesture. Tab/Enter selects a footprint. Hover a pad to see its name. Source history is separate.


Build and manufacturing scope
Source edits rebuild unrouted physical intent. Routing, copper fills and manufacturing outputs become stale; rebuild before ordering. Ratsnest is a guide, not routing.
